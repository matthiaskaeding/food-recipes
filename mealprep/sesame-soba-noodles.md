# Sesame Soba Noodles

Source: https://www.loveandlemons.com/sesame-soba-noodles/
Serves 2 to 4 · 20 min

## Ingredients

### Dressing

- 60 ml (¼ cup) rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp tamari or soy sauce, plus extra for serving
- ½ tsp toasted sesame oil, plus extra for serving
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 1 garlic clove, grated
- ½ tsp maple syrup or honey

### Noodles

- 170 g (6 oz) soba noodles
- 1 lemon wedge
- 2 avocados, sliced
- About 320 g (2 cups) blanched snap peas
- About 40 g (¼ cup) shelled, cooked edamame
- 1 watermelon radish or 2 red radishes, thinly sliced
- About 5 g (¼ cup) fresh mint leaves
- Sesame seeds

## Method

1. Whisk the dressing ingredients together.
2. Cook soba in unsalted boiling water for the package time. Drain and rinse thoroughly under cold water to remove starch and prevent clumping.
3. Toss noodles with dressing and divide between bowls.
4. Squeeze lemon over the avocado. Add avocado, peas, edamame, radish, mint, and sesame seeds to the bowls.
5. Add extra tamari or sesame oil to taste.

## Notes

Refrigerate in sealed containers for up to 4 days, adding avocado and herbs just before eating. Produce weights are approximate.
