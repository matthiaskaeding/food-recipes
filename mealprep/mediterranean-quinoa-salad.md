# Mediterranean Quinoa Salad

Source: https://www.loveandlemons.com/quinoa-salad-recipe/
Serves 6 · 40 min with quinoa and tomatoes prepared ahead; plus 2–3 h tomato roasting and quinoa cooling

## Ingredients

### Quinoa and roasted tomatoes

- 170 g (1 cup) dry quinoa, to make about 555 g (3 cups) cooked quinoa
- 420 ml (1¾ cups) water
- 450 g (3 cups) cherry tomatoes, halved
- Extra-virgin olive oil, for drizzling
- Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper
- Fresh thyme, optional

### Roasted chickpeas

- 165 g (1 cup) cooked chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- Extra-virgin olive oil, for drizzling
- Sea salt
- Paprika or other spices, optional

### Italian dressing

- 6 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 3 tbsp white wine vinegar
- 3 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1½ tbsp finely chopped fresh parsley
- 1½ tsp honey
- 1½ tsp dried oregano
- 1 garlic clove, grated
- ¾ tsp Dijon mustard
- ¾ tsp dried thyme
- Heaping ¼ tsp sea salt
- Freshly ground black pepper

### Salad

- 40 g (2 cups) arugula
- 120 g (1 cup) sliced Persian cucumbers
- About 25 g (1 cup) mixed fresh basil and mint leaves
- 110 g (¾ cup) crumbled feta cheese
- 110 g (¾ cup) pitted, sliced kalamata olives
- 80 g (½ cup) diced red onion
- 45 g (⅓ cup) toasted pine nuts
- 2 garlic cloves, grated
- ½ tsp sea salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- Red pepper flakes

## Method

1. Rinse the quinoa. Bring it and the water to a boil in a covered saucepan, reduce the heat to low, and simmer for 12–15 minutes until the water is absorbed; turn off the heat, leave covered for 10 minutes, then fluff and cool completely before assembling the salad.
2. Heat the oven to 120°C (250°F) and line a large baking sheet with parchment. Arrange the tomatoes cut side up, drizzle with olive oil, season with salt and pepper and optional thyme, and roast for 2–3 hours until shriveled around the edges; cool.
3. For the chickpeas, heat the oven to 220°C (425°F) and line a baking sheet with parchment. Pat the chickpeas thoroughly dry, remove loose skins, then toss with a drizzle of olive oil and salt and roast for 20–30 minutes until crisp; add optional spices while warm.
4. Whisk the dressing's oil, vinegar, lemon juice, parsley, honey, oregano, garlic, mustard, thyme, salt, and pepper together. Stir in the salad's 2 grated garlic cloves.
5. Combine the cooled quinoa and tomatoes with the arugula, cucumbers, basil, mint, feta, olives, onion, and pine nuts. Toss with half the dressing, then add the salad's ½ tsp salt, pepper, and a few pinches of red pepper flakes; add more dressing to taste and top with the roasted chickpeas just before serving.

## Notes

- The source's 40-minute total assumes the quinoa and slow-roasted tomatoes are ready in advance. Prepare those components up to 3 days ahead and refrigerate; cool the quinoa before mixing so it does not wilt the greens.
- For a vegan salad, omit the feta and replace the dressing's honey with maple syrup.
- Store leftover fresh herbs separately and add just before serving. Keep roasted chickpeas at room temperature, loosely covered, and use within 2 days for the best crunch.
