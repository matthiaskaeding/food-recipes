# Greek Red Lentil Soup

Source: https://www.themediterraneandish.com/red-lentil-soup-recipe/
Serves 6 · 33 min

## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp olive oil, plus extra for serving
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 3 tsp dried oregano
- 1½ tsp cumin
- 1 tsp dried rosemary
- ½ tsp chilli flakes
- 2 bay leaves
- About 240 g (1 cup) canned crushed tomatoes
- 1,680 ml (7 cups) low-sodium vegetable broth
- 400 g (2 cups) dry split red lentils, rinsed
- Kosher salt
- Zest of 1 lemon
- Juice of 2 lemons
- Fresh parsley
- Crumbled feta, optional

## Method

1. Heat oil until shimmering. Cook onion, carrots, and garlic for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring regularly.
2. Stir in oregano, cumin, rosemary, chilli, and bay leaves for a few seconds until fragrant.
3. Add tomatoes, broth, lentils, and salt. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 15 to 20 minutes until lentils are fully tender.
4. Remove from heat and discard bay leaves. Blend with an immersion blender on low speed until creamy.
5. Reheat gently and stir in lemon zest, juice, and parsley. Serve with extra oil and optional feta.

## Notes

Refrigerate in a sealed container for 3 to 4 days. Reheat with extra broth if needed.
