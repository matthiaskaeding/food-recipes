# Paprika Chicken with Chickpeas and Carrots

Source: https://www.themediterraneandish.com/sheet-pan-chicken-with-chickpeas/
Serves 8 · 40 to 45 min

## Ingredients

### Tomato mixture

- 120 ml (½ cup, about 109 g) olive oil
- 120 ml (½ cup) lemon juice, about 2 large lemons
- 5 tbsp tomato paste
- 5 large garlic cloves, minced
- 1½ tsp cumin
- 1½ tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp Aleppo pepper flakes

### Chicken and vegetables

- 1 tsp olive oil, plus extra for greasing
- 6 carrots, peeled, halved lengthwise, cut into 2-inch pieces
- 2 onions, halved and sliced ½ inch thick
- 1 × 425 g (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained
- ½ tsp kosher salt and ½ tsp black pepper, plus extra for chicken
- 10 boneless, skinless chicken thighs

## Method

1. Heat oven to 220°C (425°F). Set racks centrally and 4 to 5 inches below the grill.
2. Whisk tomato mixture ingredients together.
3. Toss vegetables and chickpeas with salt, pepper, 4 tbsp tomato mixture, and 1 tsp oil. Spread on a greased baking tray.
4. Pat chicken dry, season, and coat with remaining tomato mixture. Add to tray.
5. Roast centrally for 25 to 30 minutes until chicken reaches 74°C (165°F). Grill on the upper rack for 3 to 4 minutes until browned, watching closely.

## Notes

Refrigerate leftovers for up to 4 days; reheat in a skillet.
