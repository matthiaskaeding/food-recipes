# Spicy Canned Salmon Rice Bowl

Source: https://www.skinnytaste.com/spicy-canned-salmon-rice-bowl/
Serves 1 · 5 min; rice cooked beforehand

## Ingredients

- 1 can (142 g / 5 oz) skinless wild pink or red salmon in water, drained
- 1 tbsp light mayonnaise
- 2 tsp sriracha, plus more for topping
- 2 spring onions, chopped, white and green parts separated
- Pinch of kosher salt
- 146 g (¾ cup) cooked brown rice, heated
- 60 g (½ cup) chopped cucumber, preferably Persian
- Furikake, or chopped nori and sesame seeds, for garnish

## Method

1. Stir the drained salmon with the mayonnaise, sriracha, spring onion whites and salt in a small bowl.
2. Put the warm rice in a serving bowl and spoon the salmon mixture over it.
3. Add the cucumber and spring onion greens. Finish with furikake, or chopped nori and sesame seeds, and extra sriracha if wanted.

## Notes

- Use cooked leftover salmon instead of canned salmon if preferred.
- The 5-minute timing assumes cooked rice. Frozen cooked brown rice is a quick option.
- Refrigerate leftovers for up to 4 days.
- Cup-to-gram conversions are approximate.
