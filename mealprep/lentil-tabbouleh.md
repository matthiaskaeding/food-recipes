# Lentil Tabbouleh

Source: https://mypureplants.com/lentil-tabbouleh/
Serves 4 · 15 min

## Ingredients

- 11 oz canned brown lentils (1 can), drained
- 1 purple onion, chopped
- 9 oz cherry tomatoes, diced
- ½ large cucumber, diced
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp fresh mint, chopped
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- ½ lemon, juiced
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- 4 pita bread, to serve

## Method

1. Chop the onion. Dice the tomatoes and cucumber. Chop the mint and parsley.
2. In a large bowl, combine the lentils, onion, tomatoes, cucumber, mint, and parsley. Add the lemon juice and olive oil, season with salt and pepper, and mix well.
3. Serve with fresh pita bread.
