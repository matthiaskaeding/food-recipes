# Gigantes Plaki (Greek Baked Beans)

Source: https://www.themediterraneandish.com/gigantes-plaki-greek-giant-beans/
Serves 4 · 40 min

## Ingredients

- 30 ml (⅛ cup, about 27 g) olive oil, plus extra for serving
- ½ large onion, chopped
- 1 large carrot, peeled and chopped
- 1 celery stalk, chopped
- 2 large garlic cloves, minced
- ½ tsp kosher salt, divided, plus more to taste
- 1 × 425 g (15 oz) can diced tomatoes, including juice
- 6 tbsp water
- 7.5 g (¼ cup) chopped parsley, plus extra for serving
- ½ tbsp fresh thyme leaves
- ¾ tsp dried oregano
- ¼ to ½ tsp chilli flakes
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- ⅛ to ¼ tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 dried bay leaf
- 1 × 425 g (15 oz) can butter beans, drained and rinsed
- Crumbled feta and bread, for serving

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 190°C (375°F), with a central rack.
2. Heat oil in a large ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat. Stir-fry onion, carrot, celery, garlic, and half the salt for 5 to 7 minutes until softened.
3. Add tomatoes, water, parsley, thyme, spices, bay leaf, and remaining salt.
4. Add beans; cook over high heat for 5 minutes. Adjust seasoning, then bake for 20 to 30 minutes until thickened and lightly browned.
5. Discard bay leaf. Finish with oil, parsley, and feta; serve with bread.

## Notes

Refrigerate leftovers for up to 4 days. Other creamy white beans also work.
