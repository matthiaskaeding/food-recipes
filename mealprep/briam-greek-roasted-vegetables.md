# Briam (Greek Roasted Vegetables)

Source: https://www.themediterraneandish.com/briam-greek-roasted-vegetables/
Serves 6 or more · 20 min prep, 75 to 85 min roasting

## Ingredients

- 794 g canned diced tomatoes, including juice
- 60 ml (¼ cup, about 55 g) extra virgin olive oil, plus extra for serving
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 35 g (½ cup) chopped parsley
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- Scant 1 tsp dried rosemary
- Salt and pepper
- 570 g gold potatoes, peeled, sliced ⅛ inch thick
- 570 g zucchini, sliced ¼ inch thick
- 1 large red onion or 2 small, thinly sliced

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C), with a central rack.
2. Mix tomatoes and their juice with oil, garlic, parsley, oregano, rosemary, salt, and pepper in a large bowl. For a smoother sauce, blend the tomatoes first; the sauce does not need cooking beforehand.
3. Add potatoes, zucchini, and onion, then toss until coated.
4. Spread in an 11-inch ovenproof pan or a large, shallow baking dish. Scrape any remaining sauce over the vegetables.
5. Cover loosely with foil and bake for about 45 minutes. Uncover and bake for another 30 to 40 minutes, until the potatoes are tender, the edges are browned, and the sauce has thickened.
6. Serve warm or at room temperature with extra oil.

## Notes

The method is adapted to mix the sauce through all the vegetables before baking.

Refrigerate overnight or freeze. Thaw overnight in the fridge, then reheat covered in a moderate oven with a little water.
