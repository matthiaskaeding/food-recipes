# Mujadara

Source: https://cookieandkate.com/mujaddara-recipe/
Serves 4 · 60 min

## Ingredients

- 4 garlic cloves, peeled and smashed
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 1¾ tsp salt, divided
- Black pepper
- 1,200 ml (5 cups) water
- About 180 g (1 cup) regular brown basmati rice, rinsed
- 200 g (1 cup) dry brown or green lentils, rinsed
- 80 ml (⅓ cup, about 73 g) olive oil
- 2 medium-to-large yellow onions, thinly sliced
- About 50 g (½ cup) sliced spring onions
- 15 g (½ cup) chopped coriander or parsley
- Plain yogurt and optional chilli sauce, to serve

## Method

1. Boil water with garlic, bay, cumin, 1½ tsp salt, and pepper. Add rice; cover and simmer over medium heat for 10 minutes.
2. Add lentils; cover and simmer over medium-low heat for 20 to 23 minutes until tender.
3. Meanwhile, cook yellow onions in oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat for 20 to 30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned. Reduce heat if browning before softening.
4. Drain onions on paper towels; add remaining salt.
5. Drain excess rice water. Off heat, cover the pot with a towel and lid for 10 minutes.
6. Discard bay; mash garlic. Fold in three-quarters of the herbs and spring onions; adjust seasoning.
7. Top with browned onions and remaining herbs; serve with yogurt and sauce.

## Notes

Cook other rice or lentil varieties separately; their cooking times differ.
