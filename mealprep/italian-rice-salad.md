# Italian Rice Salad

Source: https://www.themediterraneandish.com/italian-rice-salad/
Serves 6 · 20 min, plus 30 min chilling; rice cooked beforehand

## Ingredients

### Salad

- 390 g (2 cups) cooked brown rice, or white rice
- 7 oz tuna packed in extra virgin olive oil
- 150 g (1 cup) cherry tomatoes, halved
- 160 g (1 cup) peas, thawed and cooked, or canned and drained
- ½ red onion, chopped
- ½ red bell pepper, chopped
- 6 oz marinated artichoke hearts, halved
- 2 small pickled cucumbers, chopped
- 30 g (about 1 cup) chopped fresh parsley

### Dressing

- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 2 to 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Combine the cooked rice with all the other salad ingredients in a large bowl.
2. Whisk the dressing ingredients together in a small bowl.
3. Mix the dressing through the salad and adjust the seasoning.
4. Refrigerate for 30 minutes, then toss again before serving.

## Notes

- Makes 4 lunch portions or 6 side portions.
- Refrigerate leftovers in a sealed container for 2 to 3 days.
- Cup-to-gram conversions are approximate.
