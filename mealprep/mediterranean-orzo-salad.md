# Mediterranean Orzo Salad

Source: https://www.foodiecrush.com/mediterranean-orzo-salad/
Serves 12 · 30 min, plus 1 hr chilling

## Ingredients

- 16 oz dry orzo pasta
- 90 g (3 cups) baby spinach, torn into large pieces
- 1½ red bell peppers, chopped (about 1 pepper)
- 120 g (1 cup) diced, seeded cucumber (about 1 medium)
- 120 g (¾ cup) diced red onion
- 5 oz Castelvetrano green olives, drained and halved
- 5 oz Kalamata olives, drained and halved
- 7 oz feta cheese
- 120 ml (½ cup) canola oil
- 60 ml (¼ cup) olive oil
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1½ tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil and cook the orzo for 10 minutes. Drain, rinse, and set aside to cool.
2. Transfer the cooled orzo to a large bowl. Add the spinach, bell pepper, cucumber, red onion, and both olives, then crumble half the feta over the top.
3. Whisk the canola oil, olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, salt, and pepper in a small bowl. Pour over the salad and gently fold to coat. Taste for seasoning, then top with the remaining feta.
4. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour, or overnight, before serving.

## Notes

Make-ahead friendly — it holds well in the fridge, and the flavor improves after an overnight chill.
