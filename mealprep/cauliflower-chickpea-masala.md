# Cauliflower and Chickpea Masala

Source: https://www.budgetbytes.com/easy-cauliflower-and-chickpea-masala/
Serves 4 · 40 min

## Ingredients

### Spice mix

- 2 tbsp garam masala
- ½ tsp cumin
- ½ tsp turmeric
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ¼ tsp cayenne
- ½ tsp salt
- Black pepper

### Masala

- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- ½ tbsp grated ginger
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 340 g (12 oz) frozen cauliflower florets
- 1 × 425 g (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained
- 425 g (15 oz) tomato sauce or passata
- 60 ml (¼ cup) water
- 80 ml (⅓ cup) heavy cream
- Salt, to taste

## Method

1. Combine the spice mix ingredients.
2. Cook onion, garlic, and ginger in oil over medium heat for about 3 minutes until softened. Stir in spices for 1 minute.
3. Add cauliflower and cook for about 5 minutes until thawed and coated.
4. Add chickpeas, tomato sauce, and water. Simmer over medium-low heat for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally; add water if drying out.
5. Remove from heat, stir in cream, and adjust salt. Serve with rice or bread.

## Notes

Blanch fresh cauliflower before substituting it. Refrigerate leftovers for 3 to 4 days; reheat in a skillet.
