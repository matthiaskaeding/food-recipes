# Orzo Salad

Source: https://www.loveandlemons.com/orzo-salad/
Serves 6 · 38 min

## Ingredients

### Dressing

- 60 ml (¼ cup) extra-virgin olive oil
- 4 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- ¼ tsp Dijon mustard
- ½ tsp sea salt
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- Freshly ground black pepper

### Salad

- 300 g (1½ cups) dry orzo pasta
- 2 Persian cucumbers, halved lengthwise and sliced ¼-inch thick
- 300 g (2 cups) halved cherry tomatoes
- 165 g (1 cup) cooked chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 4 oz feta cheese, cut into ¼-inch cubes
- 55 g (⅓ cup) thinly sliced red onion
- 75 g (½ cup) pitted Kalamata olives
- 20 g (1 cup) fresh basil and/or mint leaves

## Method

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the orzo per package directions until just past al dente, then drain and toss with a little olive oil so it doesn't stick together. Spread on a baking sheet to cool.
2. Whisk together the olive oil, red wine vinegar, garlic, oregano, Dijon mustard, and sea salt. Stir in the lemon juice and season with black pepper.
3. In a large bowl, toss the cooled orzo with the cucumbers, tomatoes, chickpeas, feta, red onion, and olives. Add the dressing and half the herbs, season with more black pepper, and toss to coat. Garnish with the remaining herbs before serving.

## Notes

If making ahead, hold back the herbs and stir them in just before serving.
