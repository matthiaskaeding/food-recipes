# Roasted Veggie Grain Bowl

Source: https://www.loveandlemons.com/grain-bowl/
Serves 4 · 60 min

## Ingredients

### Quinoa

- 1 cup quinoa, rinsed
- 1¾ cups water

### Sauce

- ½ cup pepitas (or shelled raw pistachios)
- 2 small garlic cloves
- 1 cup packed chopped kale
- 1 cup packed fresh cilantro, plus more for garnish
- ¼ cup fresh lemon juice
- ½ tsp sea salt, plus more to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper
- ½ cup extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for drizzling
- ½ cup water
- ½ tsp maple syrup or honey

### Roasted vegetables

- 2 parsnips, chopped into ½-inch pieces
- ½ cauliflower, broken into florets
- 1½ cups Brussels sprouts, halved
- 1 bunch broccolini
- Olive oil, salt, and pepper

### To serve

- 1 (14 oz) can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- Sauerkraut
- Toasted pepitas

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 425°F and line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
2. Combine the rinsed quinoa and water in a medium pot. Bring to a boil, cover, reduce heat, and simmer for 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let sit, covered, for 10 more minutes, then fluff with a fork.
3. Blend the pepitas, garlic, kale, cilantro, lemon juice, salt, pepper, olive oil, water, and maple syrup (or honey) until smooth.
4. Spread the parsnips, cauliflower, and Brussels sprouts on one baking sheet, and the broccolini on the other. Drizzle with olive oil, season with salt and pepper, and toss to coat. Roast the parsnip mixture for 20–25 minutes, until golden at the edges; roast the broccolini for 10–12 minutes, until tender. Once cool enough to handle, chop the broccolini stems.
5. Assemble bowls with quinoa, roasted vegetables, and chickpeas. Top with sauerkraut and pepitas, and drizzle with the sauce. Season to taste and serve.
