# Beef & Sweet Potato Meatballs (with Oats)

Source: User-provided recipe
20 min

## Ingredients

- 450 g (1 lb) ground beef
- 245 g (1 cup) mashed sweet potato
- 10 g (¼ cup) finely chopped spinach
- 25 g (¼ cup) oats (quick oats, or rolled oats finely blended)
- 1 egg
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Garlic powder, to taste (optional)

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and line a baking tray with parchment.
2. Mix the beef, sweet potato, spinach, oats, egg, olive oil, and garlic powder (if using) in a bowl until well combined.
3. Roll into small, 1-inch meatballs and arrange on the tray.
4. Bake for 20 minutes, until fully cooked through.

## Notes

- If the mixture feels too dry, add a splash of water or milk. If too wet, let it sit 2–3 minutes so the oats absorb moisture.
- Grinding the oats into flour gives a softer texture, better for younger babies.
- Keeps in the fridge for up to 4 days, or the freezer for up to 3 months.
